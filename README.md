# Two Sides, Same Coin: Joker and Goro Akechi in *Persona 5 Royal*

If you ask most fans what makes [Persona 5 Royal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persona_5_Royal) stick with you long after the credits roll, a huge chunk of them will point to the rivalry between the protagonist (**Joker**) and **Goro Akechi**. On paper, it looks like a standard cat-and-mouse setup: a phantom thief vs. a celebrity detective. In practice, it is way messier—and way better—because they are basically running on the exact same trauma, just pointed in opposite directions.

---

## Same Rigged Game, Different Hands

Both Joker and Akechi get their lives derailed by the exact same corrupt politician, **Masayoshi Shido**, and both get handed supernatural power as pawns in a rigged bet over humanity's future. What separates them is what they do once society shuts the door on them.

| Trait | Joker (Ren Amamiya) | Goro Akechi |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Public Image** | Branded a delinquent with a record | Beloved "Detective Prince" |
| **Reality** | Empathetic leader who protects people | Lonely hitman fueled by revenge |
| **Arcana** | `The Fool` (infinite potential) | `Justice` (warped by resentment) |
| **Personas** | Arsène $\rightarrow$ Satanael | Robin Hood / Loki $\rightarrow$ Hereward |
| **Support System** | Found family (Phantom Thieves & Sojiro) | None—kept everyone at arm's length |

Joker gets punished for doing the right thing, gets exiled to Tokyo, and *still* chooses to open himself up to other outcasts. Akechi grows up unwanted in the foster system, realizes the world only cares about you if you look useful, and builds a squeaky-clean media persona just to make people look his way. 

> "You're just some criminal trash living in an attic! So how...?! How does someone like you have things I don't?!"  
> — *Goro Akechi*

That outburst isn't just villain salt—it's the core of their whole dynamic. Joker is a criminal on paper who acts like a hero; Akechi is a hero on paper who acts like a monster. Yet Akechi isn't some cartoon psychopath from birth. Even the Thieves recognize that without a support system, any of them could have ended up twisted the same way. They don't excuse what he did, and they don't magically forgive him, either. The tragedy is simply timing: by the time Joker enters his life, Akechi is already in too deep.

---

## Why Their Powers Mirror Who They Are

One of the coolest details in *Persona 5* is how gameplay lore backs up the writing. Both characters hold the **Wild Card**—the rare ability to wield multiple Personas—but it plays out completely differently for each of them:

1. **Joker's growth comes from connection.** Every Confidant bond he builds makes him stronger, unlocking new fusions and culminating in Satanael when people choose to believe in him.
2. **Akechi's power is stunted by isolation.** Because he refuses to let anyone close, he never taps into other Arcana. Instead, he is stuck splitting himself between just two Personas:
   - `Robin Hood`: the flashy, righteous mask he shows the public.
   - `Loki`: the ruthless, bitter truth he keeps hidden.

In *Persona 5 Royal*, the writers made his Justice Confidant optional rather than automatic, which changes everything. You actually spend time with him—playing darts and billiards, debating in Kichijoji's Jazz Jin, and realizing that ***Joker is literally the only person Akechi doesn't bother lying to for long***[^1].

---

## The Third Semester: Dropping the Mask

Vanilla *Persona 5* ended Akechi's story on a tragic cliffhanger. *Royal* pushes it further in the third semester, when Dr. Maruki rewrites reality so everyone gets a painless, curated "happy" life.

- ~~Polite, smiling Detective Prince act~~
- Unfiltered, abrasive, brutally honest teammate

Once the fake reality kicks in, Akechi stops pretending to be nice. He is sharp-tongued, borderline unhinged in combat, and completely done performing for approval. Ironically, that makes him the most honest person in the room. Even when he knows that tearing down Maruki's world could erase his own second chance at life, Akechi refuses to live as someone else's puppet. He hates Joker, respects him more than anyone else, and would rather choose an ugly, uncertain reality on his own terms than accept a comfortable lie.

### What *Royal* Leaves Behind (Checklist of Their Rivalry)

- [x] **Rank 8 Duel:** Akechi throws his glove at Joker in Mementos, turning their tension into a real rivalry.
- [x] **Showtime Attack:** A literal neon sign behind them reads *"Two Sides, Same Coin."*[^2]
- [x] **Third Awakening:** Robin Hood and Loki fuse into *Hereward*—rebellion without the fake halo.
- [ ] **The Rematch:** Joker still carries Akechi's glove in his pocket at the end, keeping that promise open.
